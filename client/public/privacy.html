Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

SudokuRush is a cooperative sudoku game you play inside Discord (as a Discord Activity) or in your browser. This policy is short because we collect as little as possible and keep it only for the length of a game.

What we process

  • Your Discord identity (Discord Activity play). Joining inside Discord asks for the minimum identify scope: your Discord user ID, displayed username, and avatar hash. The ID identifies your seat in the lobby; the name labels it for other players.
  • A locally stored guest profile (browser play). Outside Discord, the site generates a random identifier and remembers the display name you type, in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device except as the name and random ID needed to place you in a lobby.
  • Transient game state. While a lobby is active, our server keeps the shared board, pencil marks, per-player contribution counts, and the puzzle's solution so it can judge moves. All of it is deleted when the last player leaves. We do not build accounts, profiles, or histories.
  • Standard hosting telemetry. The game runs on Cloudflare Workers, whose standard request logs may include IP addresses and timestamps for security and abuse prevention.

What we never do

  • We do not sell data or run advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We do not send email or messages of any kind.
  • We do not post to Discord on your behalf.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the project's repository issues page.
